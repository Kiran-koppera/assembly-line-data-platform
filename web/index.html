General assembly · data reliability

From station events to trusted line KPIs

A simulated 40-station general-assembly line at 60-second takt sends PLC, torque, stoppage, quality and end-of-line events. They are ingested into PostgreSQL, modelled and tested with dbt, monitored for data faults and served here. All data is simulated.

Throughput per hour

Vehicles leaving the last station against the 60-per-hour target. Dips are breaks and stoppages.

Stations

Dwell time includes time blocked by the next station. Highest p95 first: the likely bottleneck.

Fault drills

Each fault is injected into a clean simulated day; the pipeline must flag it, and nothing else.

Torque capability

Lowest Cpk joints (first attempts). 1.33 is a common minimum.
